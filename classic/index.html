Earn 2 stars to unlock the next mission. Stars come from aim, not speed.

For grown-ups

  • Keys arrive two at a time, home row first, in the same order a touch-typing course uses. Each key and the finger that presses it share a color, so the right finger is always on screen.
  • Stars reward aim, not speed: 3 stars at 95% or better, 2 stars at 85%. Two stars unlocks the next mission.
  • Speed adjusts during a mission. It eases off when spuds get through and picks up a little during long streaks. Start on Slow.
  • A mission takes 2 to 4 minutes. One or two a day is plenty. Missed spuds never end the game; they just come back.
  • Progress is saved in this browser only.
